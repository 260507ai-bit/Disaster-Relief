About Disaster Relief

Disaster relief and emergency networks provide rapid support and coordination during natural disasters, extreme weather events, and public health emergencies. Volunteers play a critical role in distributing food, setting up temporary shelters, and offering logistical support to affected communities.

Timely response and well-organized volunteer management help mitigate risks, ensure safety, and accelerate community recovery efforts.

Volunteer Requirements

  • Generally, volunteers should be 18 years or older for field deployment.
  • Volunteers must be in good physical health for physical or outdoor duties.
  • Prior training in first aid, driving, or logistics is beneficial but not mandatory.
  • Remote/online assistance options are available for phone helpline and digital operations.
  • Emergency response coordinators determine final team assignments.

Response Process

  1. Register in advance as an emergency volunteer.
  2. Receive emergency alert notifications during regional crises.
  3. Confirm your availability and deployment area.
  4. Assist in supply packing, distribution, or field operations under guidance.
  5. Participate in post-relief debriefing and community updates.
